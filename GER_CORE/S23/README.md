# Objective

S23 investigates whether spectral renormalization of the relational tidal operator is sufficient to stabilize the dynamics generated by the Relational Spectral Geometry (RSG) framework.

The experiment follows the sequence:

1. Reconstruction of the relational environment.
2. Structural audit of the relational operators.
3. Spectral refinement.
4. Spectral renormalization.
5. Dynamic validation.
6. Automatic generation of certificates and reports.

# Current Situation

The reconstructed experiment successfully completes the entire computational pipeline.

However, an important scientific discrepancy has been identified.

The historical implementation of S23 concluded that:

- linear spectral renormalization stabilizes the operator spectrum,
- but **does not stabilize the complete dynamics**.

During the current reconstruction, the resulting dynamics became stable after the spectral refinement stage.

This difference is currently under investigation.

The central question is whether the observed stabilization represents:

- a mathematically correct improvement of the operator construction,

or

- a modification of the original dynamical model.

This distinction is scientifically important and is being analyzed before the experiment is considered finalized.
